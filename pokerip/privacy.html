PokéRip Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

PokéRip is an unofficial, free, fan-made card pack simulator. It does not require an account and does not collect personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your card vault and settings are saved only in your device's local storage. This data is never sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes it.

Third-party services

To work, the app requests card data and images from TCGdex and fonts from Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address and standard request information, as with any website. We do not control their data practices.

No purchases, ads or tracking

The app contains no ads, analytics, in-app purchases or real-money features.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
